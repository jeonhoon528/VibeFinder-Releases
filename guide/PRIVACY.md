# VibeFinder Guide — Privacy Notice

Publisher: JEONHOON, VibeFinder developer. Last updated: 2026-10-18.

## Guide package

The Guide contains bundled Help, instructions, branding and an optional local text-search helper. It has no publisher-hosted API, account system, analytics endpoint or payment integration. The helper reads bundled documentation only and sends no network requests. The package does not itself transmit your conversations to a publisher server or give the publisher access to your VibeFinder installation, audio library, settings or customized shortcuts.

## Host-service processing

Questions, attachments and answers are processed by the host service, such as ChatGPT, under that service's own terms, privacy notice, account controls and retention rules. This notice does not claim that the host collects no information. Use the host's privacy and account controls for host-held conversation information; the Guide publisher cannot inspect, export or delete it for you through this package.

## Support information

Support is provided through the [VibeFinder Discord](https://discord.gg/mBjDdQDKj). If you send a support inquiry, the publisher can see the information you choose to share, such as your Discord identity, message, application version, control labels, steps and any attachments. The publisher uses it to answer the inquiry and improve or correct the product and its documentation, and does not export it into a separate customer list. Discord processes information under its own policies. Messages in public channels may also be visible to other participants.

Support messages may remain on Discord until the author or a server operator deletes them. Contact the server operator through that Discord for questions or requests to access, correct or delete publisher-held support information. Discord's own storage, backup and deletion practices are governed by Discord; deletion from a channel does not promise immediate removal from every provider backup. Avoid disclosing credentials, personal information, proprietary recordings or confidential project material in public channels.

## Public documentation

The Guide's documentation pages are hosted on GitHub. Visits are subject to GitHub's own privacy practices; this Guide package does not add a publisher analytics collector to those pages. External links are handled by the corresponding external service.

## Scope and updates

This notice covers the Guide package and its stated support arrangements, not the VibeFinder desktop application's separate features or every host's data practices. Changes to these arrangements will be reflected on this page with an updated date. See the [usage terms](TERMS.md) and [support information](README.md#support).
