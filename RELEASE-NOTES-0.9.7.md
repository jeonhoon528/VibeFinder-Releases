# VibeFinder 0.9.7 Beta

## Waveform Selection Improvements

- **Ctrl+A in Waveform:** click the waveform body or its section header, then press Ctrl+A to select the entire audio file instead of all items in the Sample list. Ctrl+A in the Sample list retains its existing behavior.
- **Double-click to select all:** double-click the waveform to select the file from beginning to end, including portions outside the current zoomed view.
- **Drag the complete selection:** after selecting the entire waveform, drag its body to an external application or DAW without holding Ctrl. Existing selection edge and fade handles remain available. Partial selections retain their existing drag behavior.

| Action | How to Use |
| --- | --- |
| Select the whole audio file | Focus **Waveform** and press **Ctrl+A**, or **double-click the waveform**. |
| Transfer the whole selection | **Click and drag the body of the fully selected waveform** to your DAW or another supported application. Ctrl is not required. |
